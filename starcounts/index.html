Real-sky check: Gaia DR3 star fields

Each sensor footprint is queried as a polygon from the CORS-enabled GAVO Data Center TAP mirror of Gaia DR3 (gaia.dr3lite); star magnitudes are converted to the selected band with the same colour relations as the model. If the service is unreachable the fields are simulated from the local Gaia count tables and labelled as such.
